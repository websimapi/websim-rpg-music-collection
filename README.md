# RPG World Explorer
  
An interactive fantasy world explorer featuring various scenes from an RPG game world with accompanying thematic music. Click on different locations to view detailed scenes and hear music that sets the atmosphere for each area.
  
## Features
  
### Interactive Map
- Click on pins to navigate to different locations in the fantasy world
- Visual map representation of the game world
  
### Scene Viewer
- High-quality scene illustrations for each location
- Descriptive text providing lore and context
- Immersive music that matches each location's atmosphere
  
### Location Types
  
1. **Castle Highspire** - The magnificent capital city with its towering castle
2. **Contested Plains** - Battlefield where heroes face their enemies
3. **Willowbrook Village** - Peaceful town where adventurers can rest
4. **Crypts of Dread** - Mysterious dungeon filled with danger
5. **Whisperwind Forest** - Magical woodland for exploration
6. **Mount Doomforge** - Volcanic lair of the final boss
7. **Warrior's Rest** - Emotional memorial site for fallen heroes
8. **Hall of Triumph** - Celebration venue for victorious adventurers
  
## Music Tracks
  
Each location features a unique music track that enhances the atmosphere:
  
- **Main Theme** - Epic orchestral theme for the capital city
- **The Clash of Steel** - Intense combat music for battles
- **Town of Serenity** - Peaceful melody for the village
- **Depths of Shadow** - Eerie music for dungeon exploration
- **Whispers of the Wild** - Adventurous forest exploration theme
- **Wrath of the Overlord** - Epic boss battle music
- **A Hero's Sacrifice** - Emotional piece for solemn moments
- **Triumph** - Celebratory fanfare for victory
  
## How to Use
  
1. Open the application in your browser
2. Click on a location card or map pin to view that scene
3. Press the Play Music button to hear the location's theme
4. Explore all the different areas of the fantasy world
  
This interactive experience can serve as inspiration for RPG game developers or as an immersive world-building tool for fantasy storytellers.
