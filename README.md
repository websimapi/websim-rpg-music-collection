# RPG Music Collection
  
A collection of atmospheric music tracks designed for RPG games. This project includes various musical themes suitable for different scenarios in a role-playing game.
  
## Available Tracks
  
1. **Main Theme** - The epic main theme for your RPG adventure
2. **Battle - The Clash of Steel** - Intense combat music for regular enemy encounters
3. **Town of Serenity** - Peaceful melody for friendly villages and towns
4. **Depths of Shadow** - Eerie music for exploring dangerous dungeons
5. **Whispers of the Wild** - Adventurous music for exploring forests and wilderness
6. **Wrath of the Overlord** - Epic music for major boss battles
7. **A Hero's Sacrifice** - Emotional piece for sad story moments
8. **Triumph** - Short celebratory fanfare for victory moments
  
## Usage
  
All tracks are provided in WAV format. You can:
  
- Play them directly in the browser using the included music player
- Download the tracks for use in your own RPG game projects
- Use them as inspiration for creating your own game music
  
## Categories
  
Tracks are organized into the following categories:
  
- **Theme**: Main musical themes that establish the game's identity
- **Combat**: Action-oriented music for battles and fights
- **Location**: Atmospheric music for specific places in the game world
- **Exploration**: Music that accompanies discovery and adventure
- **Story**: Emotional pieces for narrative moments
- **Event**: Short musical cues for specific game events
  
Feel free to use these tracks in your RPG game development projects!
